# Invoice requirements for a plan-managed or NDIA-managed support-worker invoice

Retrieved 2026-10-13. Every requirement below is quoted from an official source (NDIA, ATO or the NDIS Pricing Schedule and PAPL). Requirements that individual plan managers add vary from firm to firm and are not covered here.

## A. NDIA record-keeping page: the official checklist

Source: NDIS, "What are the record keeping requirements", https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/working-provider/reporting-and-recording-keeping/what-are-record-keeping-requirements

**Minimum identifying information.** "All record keeping should include the:"
- "participant's name"
- "participant's NDIS number"
- "date(s) the support was delivered"
- "amount and quantity or hours of the support delivered"
- "support type."

**Invoice fields.** "You need to give participants an invoice after delivering a support or service. In addition to the minimum identifying information, invoices need to include your:"
- "business name"
- "ABN"
- "participant's address (including postcode if it's for specialist disability accommodation)"
- "support item number listed in the NDIS support catalogue"
- "claim type"
- "total invoice amount"
- "GST component if applicable (most services are GST free)"
- "ABN of the third-party provider (for plan managers only)."

**Scope rules.** "Each invoice can only be for one participant. Invoices can include multiple NDIS supports."

## B. NDIA "Guide to getting paid": data needed to claim, plus plan-managed rules

Source: https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/pricing-and-payments/payments/guide-getting-paid

- Deadline: "You need to submit payment requests within 2 years after an NDIS support has been delivered."
- "To make the payment claim, you need the following information:"
  - "participant name"
  - "participant reference number or NDIS number"
  - "dates of support"
  - "support item reference number"
  - "support item price."
- Plan-managed route:
  - "You need to send invoices directly to a participant's plan manager if they have a plan manager helping them."
  - "The invoice must include a valid Australian Business Number (ABN) – unless you're exempt from providing an ABN."
  - "You must include a statement by a supplier form if you're exempt from providing an ABN." This links to https://www.ato.gov.au/forms-and-instructions/statement-by-supplier-not-quoting-an-abn

## C. PAPL 2025-26: plan-manager obligations, price limits, and itemising travel

Source: NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits 2025-26 v1.1, https://www.ndis.gov.au/media/8096/download?attachment

- p10: "Plan managers must provide the Australian Business Number (ABN) of the service provider who delivers the support for all payment requests."
- p10: "Plan managers must always ensure that a valid tax invoice is included with relevant information about the goods/services purchased for all payment requests."
- p10: "Plan managers must adhere to the arrangements prescribed in the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits, including ensuring that the prices charged by providers do not exceed the price limits. This rule applies regardless of whether the participant purchases the support from a registered or an unregistered provider."
- p24, worked travel example: "The provider's claim for this support is in three parts, which should be shown separately on their invoice to the participant and claimed for separately in the system." The three parts are the support, the travel time and the non-labour travel costs. Implication: travel time and km costs go on separate invoice lines.
- p40, GST: "Many, but not all, NDIA supports provided to NDIS participants are GST-free. ... If GST is applicable to a support, the price limit is inclusive of GST."

## D. 2026-27 Pricing Schedule: GST and units

Source: NDIS Pricing Schedule 2026-27 (effective 24 Sep 2026), https://www.ndis.gov.au/media/8841/download?attachment

- Part 1 s1.2 (p4): "Any maximum amount specified in this document already includes any Goods and Services Tax (GST)."
- Part 1 s1.3 (p4): "Claims are made in line with the relevant Unit of Measure (Unit) listed in Part 2. The maximum amounts apply per unit of measure."
- Part 4, definition of the Hour unit (p93): "Hour: used to apply up to the hourly maximum amount, where multiple hours can be billed in a single claim and the amount is calculated as a full hour or a fraction of the limit depending on the exact minutes the service was active."

## E. ATO: GST status and tax-invoice content

- **GST registration threshold.** Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst (page updated 14 Sep 2026). "You must register for GST: when your business or enterprise has a GST turnover (gross income from all businesses minus GST) of $75,000 or more (the GST threshold)."
  - Many sole-trader support workers are below the threshold and not registered for GST.
  - I found no ATO sentence on that page saying explicitly that a non-GST-registered business cannot issue a "tax invoice". Treat that point as unverified.
- **Tax invoice contents.** Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/tax-invoices (updated 18 Sep 2026). For sales under $1,000 a tax invoice must include enough information to clearly determine:
  - "Document is intended to be a tax invoice"
  - "Seller's identity"
  - "Seller's Australian business number (ABN)"
  - "Date the invoice was issued"
  - "Brief description of the items sold, including the quantity (if applicable) and the price"
  - "GST amount (if any) payable"
  - "Extent to which each sale on the invoice is a taxable sale"

  For sales of $1,000 or more: "Tax invoices for sales of $1,000 or more also need to show the buyer's identity or ABN."
- **When an NDIS supply is GST-free.** Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/your-industry/gst-and-health/national-disability-insurance-scheme. A supply to an NDIS participant is GST-free if all of these hold:
  - "the NDIS participant has a NDIS plan in effect"
  - "the supply is of reasonable and necessary supports that are specified in the statement of supports in the participant's NDIS plan"
  - "there is a written agreement between you and the NDIS participant (or another person)"
  - it is covered by the 2021 GST-free NDIS Supports Determination

  A GST-registered provider should therefore mark each line GST-free when these conditions hold. An unregistered provider charges no GST.

## F. Field checklist for the tool (each field mapped to its source)

| Field | Required? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Provider business name | Yes | NDIS record-keeping page ("business name"); ATO "Seller's identity" |
| Provider ABN | Yes (plan-managed: "must include a valid ABN" unless exempt, in which case attach the ATO Statement by a supplier) | NDIS record-keeping page; Guide to getting paid; PAPL p10 |
| Participant name | Yes | Record-keeping page; Guide to getting paid |
| Participant NDIS number | Yes | Record-keeping page; Guide to getting paid ("participant reference number or NDIS number") |
| Participant address | Listed by NDIA | Record-keeping page |
| Invoice number | **No official NDIA or ATO requirement found.** Recommended practice only; the ATO 7-detail list does not include it | n/a |
| Invoice date | Yes for a tax invoice ("Date the invoice was issued") | ATO tax invoices page |
| Support item number | Yes | Record-keeping page; Guide to getting paid |
| Date(s) of support | Yes | Record-keeping page; Guide to getting paid |
| Hours / quantity | Yes ("amount and quantity or hours") | Record-keeping page; ATO "including the quantity" |
| Unit price | Yes ("support item price") | Guide to getting paid; ATO "and the price" |
| Claim type (e.g. direct service, Provider Travel, Non-Face-to-Face, Short Notice Cancellation) | Yes | Record-keeping page ("claim type") |
| Line and invoice total | Yes ("total invoice amount") | Record-keeping page |
| GST statement | "GST component if applicable"; most NDIS supports are GST-free; limits are GST-inclusive | Record-keeping page; Schedule s1.2; ATO NDIS page |
| One participant per invoice | Yes | Record-keeping page |
| Travel on separate lines | Yes (shown separately, claimed separately) | PAPL p24 example |
| Submit within 2 years of support | Yes | Guide to getting paid |
