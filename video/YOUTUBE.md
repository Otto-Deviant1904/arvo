## Title
Arvo: offline NDIS invoicing on Gemma 4 (Hacktoberfest 2026 demo)

## Description
An independent NDIS support worker's week of shift notes goes in. Invoice lines priced against the official NDIS Pricing Schedule 2026-27 come out, plus a flag on any note that describes a restrictive practice. Gemma 4 runs on the worker's own laptop through Ollama, so no shift notes leave it. The model reads, code decides.

Code (MIT): https://github.com/Otto-Deviant1904/arvo
